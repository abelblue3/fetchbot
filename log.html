Build log

How the project came together, milestone by milestone. Dates come from our proposal and get updated as we go.

  1. Oct 25Build arm · 1a

    Order & print parts for the baseline arm

    Get the SO-101 working on its own before anything else. The hardware is an unknown quantity, and having it in hand will reveal more tasks. owner · what actually happened

  2. Nov 2Build arm · 1b

    Modify the arm to attach to the TurtleBot

    Design and print adapter pieces. Verify function and range of motion with the default LeRobot software. owner · notes

  3. Nov 9Build arm · 1c

    Test the attachment

    Check clearances and that the LiDAR still works with the arm mounted. Expect this to take longer than planned. owner · notes

  4. Nov 9Software · 2a

    Pose estimation node

    Evaluate an open-vocabulary detector, a YOLO-type detector and a VLM. Wrap the winner in a ROS node, calibrate the camera, and measure 3D localization accuracy in the lab. owner · notes

  5. Nov 9Software · 2b

    Configure MoveIt for the SO-101

    URDF + MoveIt Setup Assistant config files. Testable on any computer in simulation. owner · notes

  6. Nov 21Software · 2c

    Trajectory planner with Nav2

    Plan a route to the object's approach pose once it's localized. Needs testing on the lab robots. owner · notes

  7. TBDTesting · 3

    Integration & end-to-end trials

    Combine the separately tested arm and perception into the final stack and run trials from varied start positions. owner · notes
