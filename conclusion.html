Next steps

Future work

With more time, here's where we'd take FetchBot.

01

Autonomous search

Explore the room (frontier exploration or waypoints) when the object isn't in view, instead of only rotating in place.

02

Everyday objects

An open-vocabulary detector plus grasp-pose prediction to handle objects of any shape, not one distinctive object.

03

"Bring me the red cup"

Natural-language requests through a vision-language model or a hierarchical VLA policy on top of the same skills.

04

Wrist camera & visual servoing

Close the loop on the final centimeters of the grasp instead of relying on one estimate from far away.
